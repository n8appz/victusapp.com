QUESTIONS

Answers before you download

How do I know the numbers are accurate?

Victus resolves your meal to verified USDA FoodData Central data and other named sources, and shows you the source behind every number before you log it. You check the work instead of trusting a figure blindly — and you can edit any value.

What does it cost, and what happens after the free trial?

The core app is free forever. Victus Pro is a 7-day free trial, then $9.99/month. It renews at $9.99/month unless you cancel, and you can cancel anytime from your App Store subscriptions.

What happens to my data?

Your food log lives on your iPhone, not on our servers. No account to create, no ads, and we don't sell your data.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — Victus is iPhone-only today, with deep Apple Health integration. If you'd like an Android version, email us so we can gauge demand.

What about a restaurant meal or a dish with no clear entry?

Describe it the way you'd tell a friend — “grilled chicken burrito bowl, extra rice.” Victus breaks it into items, estimates each from its sources, and shows you what it used so you can adjust portions before logging.
